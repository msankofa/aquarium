Aquarium
Each rebuilds the scape. The first and third change where plants and blades are placed; the second builds the plant shader with or without its per-vertex glass clamp. Build times are logged to the console.
Likelihood is the chance that each duckweed patch holds a cloud of this species. Spread is how much of the patch a cloud covers. Depth is how far below the surface it reaches; it is densest against the fronds. Draw distance is how far from the camera the species is still drawn.
